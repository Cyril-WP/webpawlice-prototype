WebPawlice

One line on a client's site gives the owner a check-up of their own website, worst risk first: leaked files, keys in page code, exposed databases, old software, fake email in their name, look-alike domains. Plus a launch walk-through, a facts check and a competitor comparison. Visitors never see it.

See it on a client site Code on GitHub

Put it on a site

Paste before </body>. Nothing runs for visitors.


      

      
    
        
  1. The owner opens their own site with ?webpawlice on the end, e.g. kopiandcrumb.sg/?webpawlice.
    2. 
        
  2. A blue paw appears and runs the check-up. The result is kept for 12 hours in that browser.

WebPawlice

      

    

  


  

    
Four views in one panel

    

      
Risks
Every finding ranked critical, high, medium or low, with why it matters and the fix. Passed checks and non-security notes fold away.

      
Launch guard
Walks up to 25 pages like a visitor: dead pages and images, pages hidden from Google, staging leftovers, placeholder text, insecure forms, dead outside links.

      
Facts
Phone, hours, prices, address and reviews from every page and the search data, and every place the site contradicts itself.

      
Compare
The public-record checks for the site and up to three competitors, side by side. Example.

Read-only: it never submits forms, never logs in and never sends load. Leaked files are reported by name; their contents are never shown or kept.
